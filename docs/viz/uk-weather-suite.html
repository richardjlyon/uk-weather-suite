    The record is not evenly deep

    Hourly observations by decade. The archive reaches back to the 1870s, but the modern network — and the automation that came with it — is a creature of the last forty years. Any claim about long-run change lives or dies on the thin left-hand end.

    observations per decade

    Who changed, and who did not

    Each station twice over: its surroundings in 1975 against 2020. Points on the diagonal did not change. Points above it were built around — and those are the stations whose thermometers may be reading the town rather than the weather. The cluster hugging the origin is the control group this project needs.

    Built from the project's own Parquet tables — station coordinates and record spans from the MIDAS capability files, built-up fractions computed as area-weighted, land-masked rings over the JRC's GHSL surface. Colours are descriptive only: the classification that will decide urban from rural is pre-registered, calibrated and deposited separately, and nothing here has been through it.
